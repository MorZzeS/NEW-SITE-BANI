## Processing and verification

Photos: deliberate crops and localized blur remove faces, vehicle plates and third-party crane contacts where present; full sanitized contact sheet is retained privately. Video: V01 uses a fixed crop; V05/V06 use conservative fixed blur regions; V06 is trimmed before a worker enters the later scene. V03/V04 show the lift without readable addresses, plates or recognizable faces in the reviewed output frames.

All 1,157 output frames were exported into 13 complete frame sheets and visually reviewed. The six full source timelines were separately reviewed at two-second intervals; the complete source videos are not claimed to have received an individual full-resolution check of every frame. No raw source video is deployed.
